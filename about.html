About me

I'm a product designer who gets complex enterprise workflows working for the people who rely on them. I start by understanding how things connect: mapping relationships, weighing constraints and finding where the process actually breaks.

I work closely with engineers and stakeholders, make the call when something needs deciding, and adjust as we learn more.

I came to product design through graphic design and marketing, which taught me how to communicate visually, read audiences, and navigate organisations. Product design was where those things clicked into place.

Background

I'm originally from the Philippines and now live in Berlin, after nearly a decade in Ireland and a few years in Singapore. I like getting things done. In my first two years here I organised product design events averaging 70 attendees, got to B1 German, joined an art market, and built my own costing and profit & loss app. I recently learned to crochet too, so there has been a lot of that.
